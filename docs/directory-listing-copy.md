Real Decibel Meter is a free, privacy-focused online sound level meter that runs directly in your web browser on desktop, iPhone, and Android. It analyzes microphone audio in real time using the Web Audio API to estimate sound pressure levels in decibels.

Key Highlights:
- Frequency Weighting: Choose between A-weighting (dBA) for human ear perception and workplace safety checks, C-weighting (dBC) for loud bass and machinery peaks, or unweighted Z-weighting (dBZ).

- Microphone Calibration: Calibrate against an acoustic reference or known sound source to store custom, device-specific offset profiles.
- 100% Client-Side Privacy: All digital signal processing (RMS calculation and filtering) occurs locally on your device. Audio samples are never recorded, transmitted, or uploaded to any server.
- Session Logging & Export: Save sessions locally and export your measurements to CSV or JSON for noise complaint logs, classroom monitoring, or acoustic testing.
- No Installation: Works instantly in Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox over HTTPS without installing native apps or creating an account.

## 9. High-Traffic Q&A Feeds (Quora & Medium Answers)

Search for these high-impression questions on Quora and provide comprehensive answers:

### Question 1: *"How can I measure decibels with my phone without downloading an app?"*
**Answer Outline**:
- Modern mobile browsers (Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android) have direct access to the device's microphone through the Web Audio API.
- Recommend opening `https://realdecibelmeter.com/`, pressing Start, and allowing microphone access.
- Note the difference between raw digital audio (dBFS) and sound pressure (dB SPL), and explain why averaging (Leq) across 30–60 seconds gives a more reliable reading than chasing momentary spikes.

### Question 2: *"What is the difference between dB and dBA in sound measurement?"*
**Answer Outline**:
- Explain that dB is a logarithmic ratio of physical sound pressure, while dBA is an A-weighted filter that models human ear sensitivity by attenuating deep bass and ultra-high treble.
- Explain why safety agencies like OSHA, NIOSH, and WHO mandate dBA for workplace noise thresholds.
- Reference the full guide: `https://realdecibelmeter.com/a-weighted-decibel-meter/` and `https://realdecibelmeter.com/db-vs-dba/`.
